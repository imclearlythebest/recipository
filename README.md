# Recipository
Recipository is a web-based platform that combines a **social recipe community** with an integrated **ingredient marketplace**. Users can **publish, browse, and interact** with recipe content, and **build a personalized experience** through a following system, a trending feed, and **saved recipe collections**.

The platform's marketplace allows users to **purchase ingredients directly**, including in bulk from a recipe post. Cart management **supports discount codes**, and orders can be tracked through a structured fulfilment workflow. Content creators **earn a share of revenue generated** from orders attributed to their posts, tracked through a personal wallet.

Additional platform features include per-recipe calorie and nutritional calculations, a global contextual search with sidebar filtering, and a post-order recipe review system. The system also provides **administrative tools** for post approval, and ingredient and shop item management.
